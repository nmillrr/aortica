Everything a copilot needs

Six pillars, one shared backbone — each built for the mission.

🫀

Multi-Task AI

A ResNet backbone with cross-lead attention feeds rhythm, structural and ischaemia heads. 28 outputs are trained; untrained outputs are withheld from every response, never shown as predictions.

Model reference → 🔍

Explainability

Integrated-gradient attribution mapped to named ECG features, plus a VAE latent factor model — explanations in clinical language.

XAI reference → 📟

Edge Deployment

A distilled 0.42 MB INT8 ONNX model for ARM devices and an offline browser fallback. On-device latency has not yet been measured on hardware.

Edge reference → 🌐

Federated Learning

Flower-based training across sites without pooling raw ECGs, with equity-gating CI checks. Model updates are not yet differentially private or securely aggregated.

Federated onboarding → 🔗

EHR Integration

FHIR R4 / HL7 interoperability, DICOM SR write-back, and worklist prioritization — copilot findings flow into the clinical workflow.

REST API → 📋

Scope

What it detects — and what it doesn't yet

The architecture has 72 outputs. 28 are trained; the rest describe findings that need labels no public ECG dataset supplies — echocardiography, serum chemistry, angiography or follow-up. Those outputs are suppressed by default.

Aortica is a research tool. It is not FDA-cleared or CE-marked and must not be used to diagnose, treat or triage patients.

  • Rhythm & conduction (18)

    Atrial fibrillation and flutter, SVT, AVRT, sinus bradycardia and tachycardia, PACs, PVCs, first-, second- and third-degree AV block, LBBB, RBBB, LAFB, LPFB, WPW, paced rhythm, normal sinus rhythm.

  • Structural (4)

    Left and right ventricular hypertrophy, left and right atrial enlargement.

  • Ischaemia & repolarisation (6)

    STEMI, posterior MI, old MI, QTc prolongation, digitalis effect, early repolarisation.

  • Not yet trained

    VT/VF, Brugada, Wellens and de Winter patterns, cardiomyopathies, electrolyte disorders and every risk prediction (mortality, heart failure, AF onset, ejection fraction).

Results

Macro-AUROC across trained findings with at least 10 positives. Operating points were tuned on validation data only.

🇩🇪

PTB-XL test fold

0.941 macro-AUROC on 2,198 held-out ECGs (patients never seen in training).

🇨🇳

Chapman-Shaoxing test split

0.975 macro-AUROC on 4,507 held-out ECGs.

🇺🇸

Georgia (external)

0.894 macro-AUROC on 10,338 ECGs from Emory University Hospital — a population, device fleet and annotation process the model never trained on.

⚖️

Against a published baseline

On the six findings it shares with Ribeiro et al. (2020) — a model trained on about 2.3 million ECGs — both score a mean AUROC of 0.967 on Georgia: Aortica is ahead on sinus bradycardia and slightly behind (by 0.001–0.014) on first-degree AV block, RBBB, LBBB and sinus tachycardia, with AF tied.
